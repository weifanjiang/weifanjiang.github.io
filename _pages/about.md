I am a final-year Ph.D. candidate in Computer Science at [Harvard Systems Group](https://systems.seas.harvard.edu/), advised by Prof. [Minlan Yu](http://minlanyu.seas.harvard.edu/). My research interest is in efficient LLM inference. I was an ML Systems research intern at [Red Hat AI](https://www.redhat.com/en/artificial-intelligence) during Summer 2026, working on [Speculators](https://github.com/vllm-project/speculators/), the speculative decoding framework in the vLLM ecosystem. I was a Student Researcher at [Technical Systems & Infrastructure, Google](https://techsysinfra.google/) from 2022-2023, working on ML-driven failure diagnosis.

Previously, I received M.S. in Computer Science from [Columbia University](https://www.columbia.edu/) in May 2021, working with Prof. [Suman Jana](https://www.cs.columbia.edu/~suman/) and Prof. [Ethan Katz-Bassett](http://www.columbia.edu/~ebk2141/). I received B.S. with double majors in Computer Science and ACMS (Applied and Computational Math Sciences, Discrete Math and Algorithms option) from [University of Washington, Seattle](https://www.washington.edu) in June 2019.
