I am a second-year Ph.D. student in Computer Science at [Harvard University](https://www.harvard.edu/). I am fortunate to be advised by Prof. [Minlan Yu](http://minlanyu.seas.harvard.edu/). I am also a student researcher at [Technical Systems & Infrastructure, Google](https://techsysinfra.google/). My research interest is broadly in **computer systems** and **networking**. I am particularly interested in applying **machine learning** techniques to solve system-related problems.

Prior to Harvard, I received M.S. in Computer Science from [Columbia University](https://www.columbia.edu/) in May 2021, working with Prof. [Suman Jana](https://www.cs.columbia.edu/~suman/) and Prof. [Ethan Katz-Bassett](http://www.columbia.edu/~ebk2141/). I received B.S. with double majors in Computer Science and ACMS (Applied and Computational Math Sciences, Discrete Math and Algorithms option) from [University of Washington, Seattle](https://www.washington.edu) in June 2019.
